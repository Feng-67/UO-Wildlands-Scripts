# UO Wildlands Custom Items

## Champion Monolith

- Lists all available Champion Spawns and highlights active champs
- Allows instant teleportation to the Champion Spawn area
- Also available in the Star Room

## Transmog Potion

A unique magical consumable that allows players to alter the aesthetic appearance of their equipment without sacrificing its combat statistics. It provides a powerful way to customize a character's look while maintaining high-end performance.

- **Function:** Changes the ItemID of a "stat" item to match the appearance of a "visual" target item.
- **Compatibility:** Requires both items to share the same handedness (e.g., one-handed to one-handed).
- **Security:** The potion is **Blessed**, ensuring it stays with the player until used.
- **Usage:** The item to be changed must be in the player's backpack or currently equipped.

## Clothing Bless Deed

A valuable utility item that provides permanent protection for a character's favorite attire. It ensures that cherished clothing items remain with the player even after an unfortunate defeat in the field.

- **Function:** Permanently applies the **Blessed** loot type to a specific piece of clothing.
- **Targeting:** Upon use, it prompts the player to select a valid item from their backpack to bless.
- **Restrictions:** Only works on standard clothing; it cannot be used on **Arcane** equipment, **Prized** items, or gear that is already blessed/insured.
- **Durability:** The deed is consumed immediately upon a successful blessing.

## Powder of Fortification

The **Powder of Fortification** is an essential maintenance tool used to extend the lifespan of valuable equipment. It allows players to reinforce the structural integrity of their gear through a simple targeting process.

- **Function:** Increases the maximum and current durability of wearable items, such as weapons and armor, up to a limit of 255.
- **Charges:** Typically comes with **10 uses**, though it tracks remaining charges and deletes upon depletion.
- **Restrictions:** Cannot be used on **Brittle** items or certain jewelry unless specifically configured.
- **Usage:** Must be used from the player's backpack on items that allow repair and fortification.

## Hangover Cure

**Grizelda's Extra Strength Hangover Cure** is a specialized artifact designed to instantly counteract the effects of alcohol consumption. This potent remedy ensures adventurers can maintain their focus after a night at the local tavern.

- **Sobriety Effect:** Instantly reduces the user's Blood Alcohol Content to zero, making them sober.
- **Capacity:** Each bottle comes with **20 uses** before it is depleted and destroyed.
- **Artifact Status:** Classified as an artifact with a distinct weight of 1.0 and a unique hue (0x2D).
- **Usage Requirements:** Must be located within the player's backpack to be activated.

## Break Paralysis Potion

A vital utility item designed to restore mobility to adventurers during intense combat encounters. It mimics the functionality of classic VvV consumables to provide a reliable escape from restrictive crowd-control effects.

- **Core Utility:** Immediately removes **Paralyzed** or **Frozen** states upon consumption.
- **Visual/Audio Feedback:** Triggers a unique chemical explosion effect and sound when used.
- **Convenience:** The item is **stackable** and features a distinct hue (2543) for easy identification in a backpack.
- **Usage:** Must be located in the player's backpack to be activated.

## Cities Atlas & Exceptional Atlas

These specialized travel books serve as high-capacity tools for navigating the world, providing reliable and permanent transportation options for seasoned adventurers.

- **Enhanced Capacity:** Both items feature **100 maximum charges** and are crafted with **Exceptional quality**.
- **Loot Protection:** Both books are **Blessed**, ensuring they remain with the player upon death.
- **Pre-Loaded Destinations:** The **Cities of Britannia** book comes pre-configured with major city locations across **Trammel, Felucca, and Tokuno**.
- **Customization:** The **Runic Atlas Exceptional** provides a blank, high-capacity slate for players to mark their own custom runes.

## Power Scroll Book

A specialized storage container designed to organize and manage a player's collection of Power Scrolls. It features a custom interface that categorizes scrolls by skill type and potency.

- **Functionality:** Automatically accepts dropped Power Scrolls, internalizing them to save inventory space.
- **Capacity:** Stores scrolls across multiple categories, including Combat, Magic, Trade Skills, Bard, and Thievery.
- **Interface:** A multi-page gump displays scroll counts for tiers **105, 110, 115, and 120**.
- **Withdrawal:** Players can easily remove specific scrolls by clicking the corresponding button within the gump.

## Stat Boost Roses

Stat Roses are specialised decorative items that provide players with consumable petals to enhance specific character attributes.

- **Variants:** Includes the **Rose of Intelligence**, the **Rose of Dexterity**, and the **Rose of Trinsic** (Strength).
- **Functionality:** Each rose starts with **10 petals**; double-clicking the rose harvests all available petals into the player's backpack.
- **Petal Effects:** Consuming a petal grants a **+5 stat bonus** to the respective attribute for **5 minutes**.
- **Stacking:** Petal effects do not stack with other "Rose of Trinsic" style buffs.

## Mannequin

A placeable house decoration used to display equipment and outfits.

- Placed inside a home using a **Mannequin Deed** (owner/co-owner only)
- Can wear and display a full set of equipment
- Owner can **swap their equipped items** directly with the Mannequin's outfit
- Supports a **custom description** (up to 44 characters)
- View and **compare item/suit stats** against selected gear
- Body and appearance are **customizable**
- Can be **re-deeded** to return it to inventory, with all equipped items sent to the owner's backpack

## Pet Leash

An essential tool for animal trainers, providing a convenient way to manage and transport loyal companions through a specialized shrinking system.

- **Shrink Capability:** Allows players to shrink their controlled pets into portable items that are placed directly into the backpack.
- **Safety Features:** Prevents shrinking if the pet is dead or if it is a pack animal and its backpack contains items, ensuring no loot is lost.
- **Protection:** The leash itself is **Blessed**, meaning it remains with the player upon death.
- **Capacity:** Each leash comes pre-loaded with **100 charges**, which are consumed upon each successful use.

## Scroll of Antique to Prized

A specialized magical utility designed to preserve high-end equipment by altering its fundamental properties.

- **Core Function:** Converts an item from **Antique** status to **Prized** status, removing the accelerated durability decay.
- **Targeting:** Can be used on weapons, armor, or talismans currently equipped or located within the player's backpack.
- **Visual Feedback:** Features a distinct red hue and triggers a unique red sparkle effect and sound upon successful activation.
- **Consumption:** The scroll is single-use and is consumed immediately after the transformation is complete.

## Scroll of Decurse

A specialized magical consumable designed to purify equipment of malevolent enchantments.

- **Core Function:** Permanently removes the **Cursed** loot type from a targeted item, restoring it to a **Regular** status.
- **Targeting:** Can be used on any cursed item that is currently equipped or located within the player's backpack.
- **Visual/Audio Effects:** Activation triggers a unique Chivalry-style "Remove Curse" sound and a distinct light-blue visual particle effect.
- **Single Use:** The scroll is immediately consumed upon a successful cleansing of an item.

## Davy Jones' Iron Poker

A blessed one-handed item used to dispose of corpses at sea.

- Appears as a spear (hue 1315) but functions as a **non-combat item** — no weapon stats
- **Blessed** — will not drop on death
- Double-click to target a nearby corpse (within 2 tiles)
- Player must be **on a boat** to use it
- The targeted corpse must be **in water** (ocean or shallow water tiles)
- On success, plays a **splash effect and sound**, then **permanently deletes the corpse**
- Useful for cleaning up bodies overboard without them cluttering the water

## Lockpicking Chest

A dynamic training chest designed to help players practice and improve their Lockpicking skill at any level.

- **Adaptive Difficulty** — On double-click, the chest automatically adjusts its lock level to match the player's current Lockpicking skill, always providing a relevant challenge.
- **Auto-Relocks** — Upon successful pick, the chest magically relocks itself, allowing for continuous, uninterrupted practice.
- **Skill Range** — Supports Lockpicking from skill level 1 up to 100 (configurable beyond that with power scrolls).
- **Portable** — Movable and weighs only 4 stones, making it easy to place in a home or carry.

## Portable Trash Barrel

A movable version of the standard Trash Barrel, designed for player housing use.

- **Movable** — Can be carried in a backpack and sold on vendors, unlike regular trash barrels
- **House-locked required** — Must be locked down or secured inside a player house before it can be used
- **No backpack use** — Cannot be opened or used as storage while being carried; attempting to do so prompts a warning message
- **Standard trash function** — Once locked down, operates identically to a normal Trash Barrel, permanently destroying dropped items after three minutes

## Mark Runestone

A blessed runestone that creates a marked rune of a player's current location.

- Double click it to mark a rune - labeled as "A Marked Rune"
- No skill requirements to create a marked rune
- Withdraws 25 GP from the player's bank account for each rune created

## Resource Storage Chests

**Virtual Storage Chests** are high-capacity organizational tools designed to declutter your home. By converting physical items into a digital inventory, they allow for efficient management of vast quantities of resources without increasing your house's item count.

- **Specialized Categorization:** Available in themes like Miner, Tailor, Reagent, Wood, and Food to store specific resource types.
- **Virtual Stockpiling:** Converts dropped items into a virtual list, saving physical space and reducing house lockdowns.
- **Easy Retrieval:** Features a user-friendly interface to view totals and withdraw items directly to your backpack.
- **Security:** Must be secured or locked down in a house to function.

## Champion Power Scroll

The **Champion Power Scroll Picker** is a versatile reward item that allows players to choose their desired path of advancement. It eliminates the randomness of scroll drops by providing a direct selection interface.

- **Custom Selection:** Upon use, it opens a comprehensive menu listing over 50 different skills to choose from, including skills not normally available as 120.
- **High Potency:** Automatically generates a **120 skill cap** Power Scroll for the selected ability.
- **Ease of Use:** Delivers the chosen scroll directly into the player's backpack instantly.
- **Visuals:** Features a distinct bright red hue (33) for easy identification.

## Stat Scroll

Stat Power Scrolls are available for purchase.

## Large BOD Deed Box

Large BOD Splitting: Drop a Large BOD into the box and double-click it to instantly generate all matching Small BOD components.

- **Anti-Exploit:** Each unique Large BOD can only be split once. The box tracks the deed's unique ID to prevent infinite generation.
- **100% Banking Value:** Banking a deed now grants 100% of its point value. The previous 80% point penalty for banking Large BODs has been removed.
- **Spending Points:** Banked points are spent normally. When you claim a reward, the points are deducted from your character's total for that skill.
- **Usage Requirements:** The box must be in your backpack, bank, or secured in a house to function.

## Interior Decorator

The **Advanced Interior Decorator** tool is now available from the **Architect NPC**, featuring **Lock Down**, **Secure**, and **Release** functions for house item management.

## Weapon Strap

The Weapon Strap is a back-slot container that holds up to 10 melee weapons and provides 50% weight reduction. The Weapon Strap is Blessed. Weapons stored inside it are protected on death and never need insurance. They will not appear on the Item Insurance Menu while inside the strap.

## Undertaker's Staff

A blessed item that retrieves items from a corpse anywhere on the same map — for example, anywhere in Felucca if standing in Felucca.

- Costs 1000 Sov's (1 mil gold) and has **100 charges**, costing the equivalent of **10K per retrieval**
- **DESTROYS ALL GOLD ON THE CORPSE** in the process

## Corpse Retrieval Stone

A New Haven–based retrieval service available to ghosts.

- Costs **15K gold per retrieval**
- When a player dies, they are prompted to use the Corpse Retrieval Stone and if so are teleported to its location in New Haven
- If the prompt is closed, a player can reopen it with the in-game command `[rezstone`
- Resurrection is initiated by moving near the stone, and double clicking it opens the item retrieval option
- Retrieves items from a corpse anywhere in the game — all maps
- **DESTROYS ALL GOLD ON THE CORPSE** in the process
