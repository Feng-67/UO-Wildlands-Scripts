The Experience

Nothing game-breaking or neon—just the game as you remember. Refined systems, immersive challenge, and thoughtful additions that respect the original spirit.

  • Refinement: Systems designed to respect your time and remove grind.
  • Challenge: Felucca spawns are increased, while PvP is completely removed.
  • Custom Pets: A range of custom pets that fit perfectly into the world.

Account Creation

To maintain a secure community, account creation is processed via Discord. Simple, transparent, and built for real players.

  • Real Players: Join Discord and start by posting a simple self-introduction.
  • Fair Play: One-account-per-IP without expiry. Three auto-refresh houses.
  • Adults: Staff will not become involved in player disputes.
